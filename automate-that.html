How to play

  1. Tap a task the moment it appears to automate it.
  2. Survive to the win time. The win timer at the top counts up — reach 0:30 on Chill, 1:00 on Busy, or 2:00 on Chaos and you win the round.
  3. Don't let tasks expire — expired tasks pile up. 12 misses and you're buried before the timer runs out.
  4. Chain streaks. Every 5 automations in a row adds a +2 hr bonus. Gold tickets are worth 5 hrs.
